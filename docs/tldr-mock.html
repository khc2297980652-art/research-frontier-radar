摘要

Vision-language models such as CLIP achieve strong zero-shot recognition but degrade sharply under distribution shift at deployment time. We propose an entropy-guided test-time adaptation framework that refines a small set of continuous prompt vectors online, using only the model's own predictive entropy on unlabeled test samples as the optimization signal. The backbone remains frozen, so no source data, labels, or retraining are required. Across 15 out-of-distribution benchmarks our method improves top-1 zero-shot accuracy by 3.8% on average while adding less than 5% overhead per forward pass…
